About Editkaro.in

Cutters, colorists,
and a small growth desk.

We started as three editors sharing one machine. Today we're a distributed team of editors, colorists and strategists working across every short and long-form format.

MISSION

Make every cut earn its watch time

We believe an edit's only job is to keep the promise the first three seconds made. No filler, no template transitions for their own sake — every cut is made to hold attention and serve the story.

VISION

The edit bay every growing creator can afford

We want small teams and independent creators to have access to the same pipeline — grading, sound design, distribution strategy — that large studios take for granted.

How we got here

A short timeline

2021

Founded as a two-person freelance edit desk

Started by cutting wedding films and small business promos around Pune.

2022

First creator retainers

Took on ongoing short-form editing for three YouTube creators, then gaming highlight channels.

2024

Added the social marketing desk

Clients kept asking who should post the edits and when — so we built that team in-house.

2026

480+ projects across 9 content categories

From football highlight reels to documentary shorts, color grading to anime AMVs.
